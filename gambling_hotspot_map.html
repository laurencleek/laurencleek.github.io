How to read this map

Raw revenue highlights venues pulling the highest six-month net gaming takings.

Per-capita extraction highlights places where local extraction intensity is highest relative to population.

Use the vulnerability slider to filter up the social-risk threshold. As you raise it, metro clusters fade and regional concentrations become clearer.
